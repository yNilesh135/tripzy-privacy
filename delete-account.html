Delete your Tripzy account

Tripzy provides both in-app and web-accessible ways to request deletion of a Tripzy account.

Delete inside Tripzy

If you can sign in, open Tripzy → Profile → ACCOUNT → Delete Account & Data and confirm deletion. The server verifies the authenticated account before deleting it.

Request deletion without the app

Email tripzysupport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Tripzy Cloud account using subject Delete my Tripzy account.

Request account deletion

Tripzy may request reasonable verification. Never send passwords, OTPs or payment-card/bank credentials.

What is deleted

  • Tripzy Cloud authentication account;
  • owned cloud vehicles;
  • owned cloud fuel and expense records;
  • owned trips, trip stops and trip members; and
  • account-owned cloud relationships configured to cascade with account deletion.

What is not automatically deleted

  • a separate Guest workspace that you explicitly kept local instead of moving into the deleted account;
  • manual backup/export/report files saved outside Tripzy;
  • local data on another offline device until it is cleared there;
  • information independently retained by a service provider under its own lawful retention requirements; or
  • limited records that must be retained for security, dispute, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.

Tripzy Plus subscription

Deleting your Tripzy account does not cancel an active Tripzy Plus subscription in Google Play. If you do not want future renewals, manage or cancel Tripzy Plus in Google Play before deleting your Tripzy account.
