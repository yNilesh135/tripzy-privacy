Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026

Tripzy is a vehicle expense, fuel, mileage, trip and reporting app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Tripzy processes, how it is used, when third-party services may process information, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you enter into Tripzy

Tripzy allows you to enter information such as vehicle details, odometer readings, fuel entries, expenses, trip plans, passenger counts, budgets, notes and related records.

This app is designed as a local-first application. Tripzy does not require you to create a Tripzy account, and the vehicle, fuel, expense and trip records you enter are stored locally on your device rather than being uploaded to Tripzy-operated servers.

2. Local storage and backups

Tripzy records are primarily stored in the app's local database on your device. Tripzy disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its private app data. You can remove locally stored Tripzy data by deleting records where the app provides that option, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

Reports that you explicitly save or share outside Tripzy are controlled by you and may be backed up, copied or stored by your device, cloud-storage provider, messaging app or other service you choose.

3. Smart Transaction Detection and notification access

If you enable Smart Transaction Detection, Tripzy uses Android's Notification Access permission to inspect supported payment notifications on your device and suggest fuel, expense or trip entries. Processing is performed on-device.

Tripzy does not automatically add detected transactions to your records; you review and confirm the suggested action first. You can disable Smart Transaction Detection or revoke Notification Access from Android settings at any time.

4. Image report sharing

Tripzy can generate image-style reports from vehicle, fuel, expense and trip information stored in the app. Reports are generated only when you request them.

Report generation uses data stored on your device. If you choose to save or share a generated report using Android's sharing options, the selected app, person or service may receive the report according to the action you choose. After sharing or saving, you are responsible for how that report is stored, copied, forwarded or deleted.

5. Advertising

Tripzy uses Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads.

According to Google's disclosures, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interaction information, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising .

6. Rewarded ads and temporary ad-free access

Tripzy may offer an optional rewarded advertisement. If you voluntarily complete the rewarded ad, Tripzy can grant a temporary ad-free period during which banner ads are hidden.

The expiry time for the temporary ad-free period is stored locally on your device so that the remaining time can continue after the app is restarted.

7. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, including for eligible users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Tripzy may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and advertising consent choices.

Depending on your region and your selections, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or otherwise limited. You may be given options such as consenting, declining consent, or managing individual privacy choices.

8. Third-party services

Tripzy currently uses Google services related to advertising and consent management, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable; and
  • Android system services used for notification access, report sharing and device functionality.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

9. Data sharing and sale

Tripzy does not sell the vehicle, fuel, expense, trip or generated report data that you enter into or create through the app.

Advertising-related information may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's policies and according to the consent choices available to you.

10. Data retention

Locally entered Tripzy data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or it is otherwise removed through your device's storage controls.

Generated reports that you save or share outside Tripzy may remain with the location, app, person or service you selected until they are deleted there.

Third-party providers may retain advertising or diagnostic information according to their own retention policies.

11. Security

Tripzy is designed to keep your core vehicle and expense records local to your device. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect access to your device and any reports you save or share.

12. Children's privacy

Tripzy is not designed or directed specifically to children under 13. If you believe that information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us using the details below.

13. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process information on servers located in countries other than your own, subject to their legal and contractual safeguards.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Tripzy features, advertising practices, legal requirements or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions about Tripzy, contact: tripzysupport@gmail.com
