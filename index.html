Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

Tripzy is a vehicle expense, fuel, mileage, trip and reporting app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Tripzy processes, how it is used, when third-party services may process information, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you enter into Tripzy

Tripzy allows you to enter information such as vehicle details, vehicle photos, odometer readings, fuel entries, expenses, trip plans, passenger counts, budgets, notes and related records.

Tripzy is designed as a local-first application. Tripzy does not require you to create a Tripzy account, and the vehicle, fuel, expense and trip records you enter are stored locally on your device rather than being uploaded to Tripzy-operated servers.

2. Local storage and automatic Android backup

Your Tripzy records are primarily stored in the app's local database and internal app storage on your device.

Tripzy disables Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its internal database, internal files and app preferences.

You can remove locally stored Tripzy data by deleting records where the app provides that option, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

3. Manual backup, restore and data export

Tripzy includes user-initiated backup, restore and data export features. These features use JSON files created only when you request them through Android's file picker.

A Tripzy backup can include your vehicle, trip, fuel and expense records, selected app preferences, and available vehicle photos. A general Tripzy data export contains the supported app data records for portability or review.

Smart Transaction pending candidates and Tripzy's temporary notification inbox/delivery bookkeeping are not included in the normal Tripzy backup/export dataset.

Backup or export files are saved only to the location you choose. After saving a file, you are responsible for how it is stored, copied, shared, backed up or deleted by the selected storage provider.

4. Smart Transactions and Notification Access

Smart Transactions is an optional feature. If you enable it, Android requires you to grant Tripzy Notification Access in system settings.

Notification Access is a powerful Android permission that can make notification content from apps on your device available to Tripzy while the permission is enabled. Tripzy uses this access on-device to look for likely outgoing payment notifications and suggest possible fuel, vehicle-expense or trip-expense records for your review.

Tripzy filters and parses supported payment-related notification sources locally on your device. Payment candidates that Tripzy identifies may include information such as the payment amount, merchant or payee name, source app, reference information, detected time, suggested category and related matching data needed to avoid duplicate suggestions.

Smart Transaction candidates are stored locally in Tripzy until you review or ignore them, or until the app removes older unreviewed candidates as part of its local cleanup process. Tripzy does not upload your Smart Transaction notification content to Tripzy-operated servers.

You can disable Smart Transactions in Tripzy and revoke Notification Access at any time through Android system settings.

5. Notifications and trip reminders

Tripzy may request Android notification permission so it can show app notifications, Smart Transaction review prompts and trip reminders that you enable.

Trip reminders are created on your device using Android system scheduling. Tripzy also listens for device reboot, app update and time/time-zone changes so opted-in reminders can be rebuilt when needed.

6. Advertising

Tripzy uses Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads.

According to Google's disclosures, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and ad interaction information, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

7. Rewarded ads and temporary ad-free access

Tripzy may offer an optional rewarded advertisement. If you voluntarily complete the rewarded ad, Tripzy can grant a temporary ad-free period during which banner ads are hidden.

The expiry time for the temporary ad-free period is stored locally on your device so that the remaining time can continue after the app is restarted.

8. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, including for eligible users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Tripzy may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and advertising consent choices.

Depending on your region and your selections, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or otherwise limited.

9. Third-party services

Tripzy currently uses services or system components including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable;
  • Android Notification Access and notification services, when you enable related features;
  • Android system file picker/storage services for user-requested backup and export files; and
  • Android system scheduling services for local reminders.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

10. Data sharing and sale

Tripzy does not sell the vehicle, fuel, expense, trip, Smart Transaction candidate or backup/export data that you enter or generate in the app.

Tripzy does not operate a server that receives your locally stored vehicle, fuel, expense, trip or Smart Transaction notification content.

Advertising-related information may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's policies and according to the consent choices available to you.

11. Data retention

Locally entered Tripzy data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, restore a different backup, or it is otherwise removed through app/device storage controls.

Unreviewed Smart Transaction candidates may also be removed automatically by Tripzy's local cleanup process.

Files that you manually export or back up remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them or the storage provider removes them.

Third-party providers may retain advertising or diagnostic information according to their own retention policies.

12. Security

Tripzy is designed to keep your core vehicle, expense, trip and Smart Transaction data local to your device. Internal automatic Android backup is disabled, and generated report sharing is restricted to temporary app-controlled files shared through Android's protected content-sharing mechanism.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect access to your device and to any backup, export or report files that you choose to save or share.

13. Children's privacy

Tripzy is not designed or directed specifically to children under 13. If you believe that information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us using the details below.

14. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process advertising-related information on servers located in countries other than your own, subject to their legal and contractual safeguards.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Tripzy features, permissions, advertising practices, legal requirements or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at Tripzy's privacy-policy URL with an updated effective date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions about Tripzy, contact: tripzysupport@gmail.com
